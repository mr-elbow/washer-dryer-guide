Maintenance & cleaning

Tub Clean cycle monthly with a cleaning tablet or hot water + bleach. Drain pump filter: front bottom-left, behind a small cover — unclip the emergency drain hose, drain residual water into a bowl, twist the filter counter-clockwise, brush clean, refit. No tools, no moving the machine. Detergent drawer (4 trays: prewash, main wash, bleach, softener) pulls out for rinsing. Dryer lint filter: pull-up, clean each load, wash in warm soapy water if clogged with softener residue; FlowSense flags restricted airflow. Gasket care is non-negotiable — wipe after use and leave the door slightly ajar, or mold builds in the gasket folds where Tub Clean can't reach. The washer door is not reversible (hinge on the left). Use 1–2 tablespoons of HE detergent per load — oversudsing is a common cause of residue and service calls.

Your layout: vent on the left, hookups on the right

This is the requirement that narrows the field, and all three picks clear it. Every LG electric dryer here has 4-way venting — rear, left, right, and bottom. The dryer ships configured for rear venting, so the left-side conversion is a kit install, not a different model.

Left-side vent kit — same for all three picks

LG Side Venting Kit, part #3911EZ9131X (replaces older part #383EEL9001B). $69.99 at Best Buy — verified in stock Nov 11, 2026. Also ~$79–81 at Home Depot parts / PartSelect. It lets the dryer exhaust from the left side, right side, or bottom. The only side restriction in LG manuals is that gas models can't vent right — these are electric, so the left side is fully supported.

Critical: the kit must be installed before the dryer is stacked — it requires access to the side knockout and blower housing, which means partial disassembly. Tell the installer in advance: "Left-side vent, kit 3911EZ9131X, the vent is on the left — please convert before stacking." One kit reviewer noted Home Depot will not install it — expect DIY or an independent installer.

Fit check for your space

  • Width: 27" per unit. Allow 1" clearance each side (LG recommends 2" for service access on the 6700).
  • Depth: plan ~33.5–34¼" from the wall with hoses and vent connected. The 6700 washer is 33⅛" deep before hoses — the deepest of the three.
  • Height: stacked 77½" for the 3400/4000; ~79" for the 6700. Measure your ceiling and check that everyone who does laundry can reach the dryer controls at the top.
  • Floor: LG stresses the floor under stacked units must be "incredibly rigid."
  • Closet installs: need an upper vent (min 48 sq in, no lower than 6 ft) and a lower vent (min 24 sq in, no more than 1 ft above the floor).
  • Hookups on the right: water/drain on the right side is fine — hoses connect at the back of the washer and reach across. The dryer vent exits left, so the two runs don't cross.
  • Washer door: not reversible on the 3400/4000 (hinge on the left). Factor that into how the pair sits in the room.

Why LG, not Samsung

The household's other appliances are Samsung Bespoke, so Samsung was the natural first look — and Samsung is cheaper in every tier. It loses on the thing that matters most here: reliability and service.

  • Yale Appliance 2025–26 service data (33,190 first-year service calls): LG front-load washers, 2.7% first-year service rate — lowest in the category. Samsung's last reported front-load rate was 4.9% (2023); Yale stopped carrying Samsung laundry and Samsung is absent from its 2026 washer data. Yale: "LG has proven to be more reliable over time and has better service coverage than Samsung" and "Samsung relies largely on independent servicers."
  • Consumer Reports, Best Washing Machine Brands of 2026: LG brand score 84 vs Samsung 77. LG occupies eight of the top 10 front-load slots.
  • Repair-tech surveys: Samsung was voted "least reliable washer/dryer" by a wide margin (8 votes vs LG's 3 in one survey of repair pros).
  • Owner-report pattern on Samsung: drum bearing / drum-support failure and control-board (PCB) failures at ~3 years, just after warranty — e.g. a ConsumerAffairs report from Apr 2026 on a 3-year-old WF45T6000A with a failed drum bearing and broken drum support.
  • J.D. Power 2025 is the one measure where Samsung looks near-average (84 problems per 100 vs GE 83, Whirlpool 88) — but Yale first-year service data and CR lab/owner scores are the stronger signals and both favor LG.
